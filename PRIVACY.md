# SmartBoot Response Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

SmartBoot Response ("SmartBoot," "we," "our," or "us") is a Roblox-focused alert notification and communication service operated by **Midnight Labs**, a Michigan-based technology company.

This Policy explains how SmartBoot collects, uses, stores, and shares information when Roblox experience owners, community operators, account holders, and authorized users use SmartBoot's Discord integrations, automated telephone calls, text-to-speech features, and related services.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use SmartBoot, we may process:

- **Discord information:** Discord user IDs, usernames, server IDs, and related information needed to connect and manage accounts.
- **Account information:** SmartBoot account identifiers, verification status, account settings, and selected text-to-speech voice.
- **Telephone information:** Phone numbers or PBX extensions submitted for verification and alert delivery.
- **Verification information:** Temporary verification codes and verification cooldown records.
- **Alert information:** Alert text, event names, place and system names, zone details, transaction identifiers, timestamps, and delivery status.
- **Roblox-related information:** Roblox Place IDs or experience links when included in a demo alert or other supported integration. SmartBoot does not require Roblox passwords, session cookies, or account credentials.
- **Technical information:** IP addresses, request details, error reports, and service logs generated when the Service is used.

## 3. How We Use Information

We process information to:

- Register and verify SmartBoot accounts and telephone destinations.
- Deliver automated voice alerts and text-to-speech messages.
- Connect alerts to authorized Discord accounts and servers.
- Process alert events and maintain delivery history.
- Operate supported Roblox-related integrations and demo features.
- Prevent duplicate, abusive, or unauthorized requests.
- Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve SmartBoot.

## 4. Telephone Calls and Recipient Interaction

SmartBoot may place automated calls to telephone numbers submitted by authorized users. Calls may contain generated speech based on alert information.

Recipients may use telephone keypad options, such as pressing 1 to repeat a message. These selections may be processed as part of delivering the alert.

SmartBoot does not guarantee that a call will be placed successfully, answered, heard, or understood. Users and organizations are responsible for having the legal authority and any required consent to contact submitted telephone numbers.

## 5. Roblox and Discord

SmartBoot is an independent third-party service. It is not owned by, operated by, sponsored by, or officially endorsed by Roblox Corporation or Discord Inc.

Roblox and Discord are separate services and may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. SmartBoot does not request or need Roblox passwords, Roblox session cookies, or Discord passwords.

## 6. How We Share Information

Midnight Labs does not sell personal information.

We may share or transmit information only as reasonably necessary to operate SmartBoot, including with:

- Discord, to provide bot and account integration features.
- Telecommunications and PBX providers, to place automated calls.
- Hosting and infrastructure providers, to operate the Service.
- Security and monitoring providers, where used to protect and maintain the Service.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the Service or its users, or to investigate suspected fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain account, verification, alert-history, and technical information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate SmartBoot, maintain security and service records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Some information may remain in backups or system logs for a limited period after it is deleted from active systems. Retention periods may vary by data type and operational requirements.

## 8. Security

Midnight Labs uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No electronic system, storage method, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their Discord accounts, SmartBoot access, API credentials, and any systems they connect to the Service.

Organizations using SmartBoot in connection with experiences or communities that involve minors are responsible for complying with applicable privacy, consent, and child-protection requirements.
